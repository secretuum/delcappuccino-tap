Privacy Policy

of the del Cappuccino app. Version of 31.10.2026.

This translation is provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Russian text prevails.

This policy explains what personal data of guests we collect, why, where we store it and how a guest can manage it. The policy is drawn up under the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan No. 94-V of 21 May 2013 “On Personal Data and Their Protection” (the “Law”).

1. Who processes the data

The owner and operator of the database containing personal data is ТОО "диси трейд", BIN 260440023739, address: Республика Казахстан, Алматинская область, город Алматы, Бостандыкский район, улица Утепова, дом 31 (“we”). Personal data enquiries: delcoffeeapp@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

  • Mobile phone number — to sign in to the App, to hand over orders and to contact the guest about an order.
  • Number confirmation data — the number is confirmed via WhatsApp or Telegram (the available methods are shown on the sign-in screen). When signing in via WhatsApp — the number from which the guest sent us the message with a one-time code, and the time it was sent. When signing in via Telegram — the phone number the guest shared with the bot, the time and the Telegram user ID in hashed form (an irreversible transformation). We do not store the profile name or other information the messenger sends along with the message. This data is needed only to make sure the number belongs to the guest.
  • Name — optional, to address the guest in the App and at the coffee shop when handing over the order.
  • Order and subscription history — what was ordered, at which coffee shop, when, the amount, the payment status and the remaining subscription cups.
  • Payment information from the bank — the amount, the payment status, its reference at the bank and, if the bank provides them, the masked card number (for example, the first 6 and last 4 digits) and the card type. We do not receive or store the full card number, expiry date or CVV/CVC, and we do not save the card for future payments.
  • Technical data — a device identifier created by the App (to protect sign-in), the IP address and server error logs. They are needed for the App to work and to be secure.

Phone location

With the guest’s permission, the App determines the phone’s approximate location — only while the App is in use — to show the nearest coffee shop. Distances to coffee shops are calculated on the phone itself: we do not receive the coordinates, they are not sent to our server or to third parties and are not stored anywhere. The guest may decline the permission — the coffee shop can be chosen manually — and may withdraw it at any time in the phone settings.

What we do not collect

We do not receive or read the guest’s other chats or their WhatsApp and Telegram contacts. We do not request phone contacts, photos, identity document details or the IIN. If new data is needed in the future, we will update this policy and, where the Law requires, ask for separate consent.

3. Why we process the data

  • registration and sign-in to the App;
  • accepting, preparing and handing over orders at the selected coffee shop;
  • accepting payments, refunds, keeping track of subscriptions and deducting cups;
  • responding to guests’ enquiries;
  • complying with legal requirements (accounting and tax records);
  • fraud prevention and keeping the App running.

We do not send advertising messages without the guest’s separate consent.

4. Legal basis

We process data on the basis of the consent the guest gives when registering in the App and for the performance of the agreement under the public offer. Consent may be withdrawn (section 8), but without a phone number and order history the App cannot accept orders.

5. Where the data is stored

Our database with guests’ personal data — the account, phone number, order history, subscription and its use, consents — is located on servers in the Republic of Kazakhstan (hosting provider ps.kz), as required by the Law. The phone number and name are stored in the database in encrypted form; a hash of the number is used for searching. Certain data is received and stored by the recipients listed in section 6 — only to the extent stated there; the number and name of every guest who has signed in, and for subscribers also the cup balance, are also stored in the iiko loyalty system (iikoCard).

6. Who we share the data with

Guests’ data is received only by the recipients listed below — to the extent needed for the specific task. We do not share data with any other third parties.

  • the coffee shops’ accounting system (iiko) — each order: items, coffee shop, pickup time, amount, payment method (card or subscription), the guest’s phone number and name, if provided — for handing over the order. In the order the guest is passed as a one-time guest. iiko keeps the orders received as sales records of the coffee shop;
  • the iiko loyalty system (iikoCard) — every guest who signs in to the App is registered there as a guest: phone number and name (if provided). If the guest has bought a subscription, an account with the cup balance and its transactions is also kept there (credit at the start of the subscription term, deduction of a cup for an order, return of a cup, write-off of the balance at the end of the term). iiko processes and stores this data on behalf of the Seller — to keep records of guests in the coffee shops’ system and of subscription cups. iikoCard advertising and informational messages are not enabled for the guest. The subscription term and its payment are recorded in our database;
  • the partner bank shown on the payment page — the amount and order number for payment and refunds; the guest enters card details directly on the bank’s page;
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) — if the guest signs in via WhatsApp. WhatsApp is connected directly through the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API), without intermediaries. When signing in, the guest writes to our WhatsApp Business number themselves. Meta sees the guest’s number, the message with the code, the time and what the guest wrote to our number, and processes this under its own rules on its own servers. We reply to the guest in the same chat only about sign-in (including the “STOP” reply);
  • Telegram — if the guest signs in via Telegram: when signing in through our bot, Telegram sees the guest’s chat with the bot and the number the guest shared;
  • the App update service (Expo) — when checking for updates, the App contacts Expo servers; they see the IP address and technical device data. The number, name and orders are not sent there;
  • the hosting provider ps.kz — the servers on which our database is stored, in the Republic of Kazakhstan;
  • government authorities — only in cases expressly provided for by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

We do not sell personal data or share it for third-party advertising.

7. How long we keep the data

  • Account data and order history — for as long as the account exists.
  • After the account is deleted, the number, name and sign-in data are deleted, and the order and subscription history is depersonalised (section 9).
  • Payment records and receipts that we are required to keep under tax and accounting legislation are kept for 5 years and then deleted.
  • Technical logs — no longer than 90 days. Phone numbers, names and sign-in codes are not written to them in plain form.

8. Guest rights

Under the Law, the guest has the right to:

  • know whether we hold their personal data and receive information about it: what is processed, for what purpose, how and to whom it is transferred;
  • require their data to be changed and supplemented where there are grounds for this;
  • require the data to be blocked or destroyed if it was collected or is processed in breach of the Law;
  • withdraw consent to the collection and processing of data, except where this contradicts the law or there is an outstanding obligation;
  • protect their rights before the authorised personal data protection body and in court.

To exercise a right, the guest writes to delcoffeeapp@gmail.com and states the phone number linked to the account. We respond within 3 working days. To make sure the request comes from the account holder, we may ask to confirm the number via WhatsApp or Telegram, as when signing in.

9. How to delete the account

In the App

“Profile” («Профиль») → “Delete account” («Удалить аккаунт») → confirm the deletion. The account is deleted immediately. If the guest has an active subscription, the App will warn that it will end. To receive a refund under section 8 of the offer, contact support before deleting the account.

Without the App

Write to delcoffeeapp@gmail.com with the subject “Delete account” and state the account’s phone number. We will ask you to confirm the number via WhatsApp or Telegram and will delete the account within 10 calendar days.

What happens after deletion

The phone number, name and sign-in confirmation data (including the hashed Telegram ID) are deleted from our database, and all sessions on devices are closed. The order history, subscription and payment records remain without a link to the number and name — depersonalised. Payment records are kept for the periods in section 7. Orders already sent to the coffee shops’ accounting system (iiko) remain there as sales records — together with the phone number and name stated in the order. The guest’s card in the iiko loyalty system (iikoCard), with the number, name and history of cup transactions (if the guest bought a subscription), remains with iiko; the link to the account in our database is deleted. The chat with our WhatsApp number and Telegram bot remains in the guest’s messenger, and the guest can delete it themselves. A deleted account cannot be restored; the guest may register again.

10. How we protect the data

  • Data is transmitted over a secure connection (HTTPS).
  • Only employees who need it for their work have access to the database, and only through personal accounts.
  • Phone numbers and names are stored in the database in encrypted form and do not appear in technical logs in plain form.
  • After a sign-in on a new device, we send a message about it to the WhatsApp or Telegram chat. If the sign-in was not made by the guest, they reply “STOP” — and all App sessions for their number are closed.
  • We notify the authorised body and guests of personal data security breaches in the manner and within the time limits established by the Law.

11. Children

The App is intended for persons aged 14 and over. If we learn that an account was created by a child under this age without the consent of a legal representative, we will delete the account.

12. Changes to the policy

We publish the new version in the App and on the website https://tap.delcappuccino.kz. We notify of material changes in the App in advance.

13. Contacts

Personal data questions: delcoffeeapp@gmail.com. Written requests: ТОО "диси трейд", Республика Казахстан, Алматинская область, город Алматы, Бостандыкский район, улица Утепова, дом 31. Support: +7 747 923 7611, delcoffeeapp@gmail.com.
