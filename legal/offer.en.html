Public Offer

for the sale of drinks and food for pickup and of the coffee subscription through the del Cappuccino app. Version of 31.10.2026.

This translation is provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Russian text prevails.

1. General provisions

  1. ТОО "диси трейд", BIN 260440023739 (the “Seller”), offers any individual who is entitled to enter into such transactions (the “Guest”) to conclude a sale and purchase agreement on the terms of this offer in accordance with the Civil Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
  2. The offer is accepted by paying for an order or a subscription in the App. By pressing the payment button, the Guest confirms that they have read and agree to the offer and the privacy policy.
  3. The current version of the offer is always available in the App and on the website https://tap.delcappuccino.kz.

2. Definitions

  • App means the del Cappuccino mobile application for iOS and Android.
  • Coffee Shop means the Seller’s coffee shop where the Guest collects the order. Addresses and opening hours of the Coffee Shops are shown in the App.
  • Order means a set of drinks and food from the menu placed in the App for collection at the selected Coffee Shop (pickup).
  • Subscription means a prepaid allowance of 30 cups of drinks for 30 calendar days under the rules of section 8.
  • Bank means the Seller’s partner bank shown on the payment page, which accepts online payments on behalf of the Seller.

3. Registration

  1. To place an order, the Guest signs in to the App with a mobile phone number. The number is confirmed via WhatsApp or Telegram; the available methods are shown on the sign-in screen.
  2. Via WhatsApp: the App opens WhatsApp with a ready message containing a one-time code, and the Guest sends it from their own number to the Seller’s number. Via Telegram: the App opens the Seller’s bot, and the Guest shares their own number with it. The link and the code are single-use and valid for a few minutes — the time limit is shown in the App. After sign-in, the Seller replies in the same chat that a sign-in on a new device has taken place.
  3. If the Guest receives a sign-in message they did not initiate, they reply “STOP” («СТОП») in the same WhatsApp or Telegram chat: all App sessions for their number are closed. The Guest can sign in again from the App.
  4. One phone number — one account. The Guest is responsible for keeping access to their phone secure; actions performed after the number is confirmed are deemed to be performed by the Guest.
  5. The App may be used by persons aged 14 and over.

4. Orders for pickup

  1. The Guest selects a Coffee Shop, menu items, size and extras, checks the cart and the total and pays for the order.
  2. The order is accepted by the Seller after successful payment (a subscription order — after a cup is deducted from the subscription and any surcharge is paid, section 8): the Guest sees the order number and its status in the App.
  3. The ready time shown in the App is approximate and depends on how busy the Coffee Shop is.
  4. The Guest collects the order at the selected Coffee Shop by giving the order number from the App. Delivery is not provided.
  5. A ready order is kept at the Coffee Shop for 2 hours after the “Ready” status appears in the App, but no later than the Coffee Shop’s closing time that day. If the Guest does not collect the order within this time, the order is deemed fulfilled and the payment is not refunded.

5. Prices and payment

  1. Prices are shown in the App in tenge and apply at the time the order is placed.
  2. Orders and subscriptions are paid in full in advance, online: by bank card, Apple Pay or Google Pay on the Bank’s payment page. A subscription cup is already paid as part of the subscription; when ordering, only the surcharge for syrups and extras is paid (clause 8.6).
  3. Payment on collection at the Coffee Shop is not available for orders placed in the App.
  4. The Seller issues a receipt to the Guest in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

6. Payment security

  1. The Guest enters card details only on the Bank’s secure payment page. Data transmission is encrypted, and the payment is confirmed with 3-D Secure if required by the card-issuing bank.
  2. The Seller does not receive or store the full card number, its expiry date or the CVV/CVC code.
  3. The Seller never asks for card details or a sign-in code by phone, messenger or e-mail. The Guest sends the sign-in code themselves — in WhatsApp opened by the App, and only to the number shown in the App. When signing in via Telegram, the Guest opens the bot from the link in the App and shares their own number with it.

7. Order cancellation and refunds

  1. A paid order is sent to the Coffee Shop immediately; there is no cancel button in the App. If the Guest changes their mind, they contact the Coffee Shop or support as soon as possible (contacts). If the Coffee Shop has not started preparing the order, the Seller cancels it and refunds the full amount.
  2. Once preparation has started, the order cannot be cancelled: drinks are made to the Guest’s choice. In disputed cases the Guest contacts support.
  3. If the Coffee Shop does not accept the order (an item is sold out, the Coffee Shop is closed, a connection failure), the order is cancelled and the full amount is refunded — as a rule, automatically. If the order is cancelled at the Coffee Shop after it was accepted, the Seller refunds the full amount on its own initiative, without a request from the Guest; the App shows that the order has been cancelled.
  4. The Guest raises a quality complaint about a drink or food at the Coffee Shop upon collection. The Seller replaces the item or refunds it.
  5. Refunds are made through the Bank by the same method used to pay for the order. The Seller processes the refund no later than 10 calendar days from the order cancellation or the Guest’s request; the time it takes for the money to reach the card depends on the card-issuing bank. Online payments are not refunded in cash.
  6. To request a refund, the Guest writes to support (contacts) and gives the order number. There is no need to provide the card number.

8. Coffee subscription

What is included

  1. A subscription is an allowance of 30 cups of drinks from the list shown on the subscription screen in the App for a term of 30 calendar days. The subscription price is shown in the App before payment. The cup balance is kept on the Guest’s account in the Seller’s loyalty system (iikoCard): on the first day of the term the account is credited with 30 cups, each subscription cup is deducted from it, and cups unused by the end of the term are written off (clause 8.4).
  2. The term starts on the day of successful payment: the payment day is the first day of the term. The term ends at 23:59 Almaty time on the 30th calendar day. If the Guest already has an active subscription, the new (prepaid) one starts on the day after the active one ends, or earlier — on the day when all 30 cups of the active one have been used, whichever comes first; if all cups have already been used at the time of payment, it starts on the payment day. The 30-day term of the new subscription runs from that day. An active subscription in which all cups have been used has no unused cups, so no refund under clause 8.16 is due for it. No more than one next subscription may be paid in advance.
  3. A subscription allows no more than two cups per calendar day (from 00:00 to 23:59 Almaty time). The limit applies per Guest: on the day a prepaid subscription starts (clause 8.2), no more than two cups in total across both. Cups not collected on one day can be collected on other days of the term.
  4. Cups not used by the end of the term expire and are not carried over to the next term. This rule does not deprive the Guest of the right to cancel the subscription: on cancellation, the money for unused cups is refunded under clause 8.16.

How to get a drink

  1. The Guest receives a subscription drink only by placing an order in the App: they choose a drink from the list and a Coffee Shop and place the order. One cup — one order: one cup is deducted from the subscription.
  2. The subscription covers drinks from the list on the subscription screen in a 300 ml size (for drinks offered in one size only — in that size). As of this version, the list includes espresso, americano, cappuccino, latte and flat white. Syrups and extras are paid as a surcharge by card when placing the order, at menu prices.
  3. If the Coffee Shop does not accept a subscription order (clause 7.3), the cup is returned to the subscription immediately and the surcharge is refunded in full. If a subscription order is cancelled at the Coffee Shop after acceptance (clauses 7.1, 7.3), the Seller returns the cup to the subscription and refunds the surcharge under section 7. Following a quality complaint (clause 7.4), the subscription drink is replaced without a new surcharge.
  4. The subscription is linked to the Guest’s account and cannot be transferred to other persons.
  5. If the Guest could not receive a drink through the Seller’s fault (the Coffee Shop was closed outside its schedule, no drinks from the list were available) and as a result cups expired at the end of the term, the Seller, at the Guest’s request, returns those cups to a subscription — the active or the next one. If the Guest has no new subscription, the Seller extends the subscription term by the number of days needed to collect those cups (no more than two per day). Such cups are not refunded in money.

Renewal

  1. The subscription does not renew automatically. When the term ends, the Guest may pay for a new subscription in the App if they wish.
  2. Before payment, the App shows: the price, the term, the number of cups and the limit of two per day, the list of drinks and the size, that syrups and extras are paid separately, that cups unused by the end of the term expire, and that the subscription does not renew by itself.
  3. The end date of the term and the number of remaining cups are shown on the subscription screen in the App.
  4. Card details are not stored for future payments: the Guest pays for each subscription themselves on the Bank’s page, without automatic charges.
  5. A new subscription is paid at the price in effect on the payment day.

Cancelling the subscription and refunds

  1. The Guest may cancel the subscription at any time by writing to support. The subscription ends on the day of the request.
  2. The Seller refunds the subscription price divided by 30 and multiplied by the number of unused cups, in the manner set out in clause 7.5. If a new subscription has been paid in advance and its term has not yet started (clause 8.2), its price is refunded in full.
  3. If the Guest deletes their account while a subscription is active, the subscription ends. The App warns about this before deletion. To receive money for unused cups (clause 8.16), the Guest contacts support before deleting the account.

9. Liability

  1. The Seller is responsible for the quality of drinks and food and for fulfilling paid orders in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
  2. The Seller is not liable for failures caused by mobile networks, the internet, app stores or the Guest’s card-issuing bank, but helps the Guest resolve the issue and refunds an unfulfilled order.
  3. The parties are released from liability in the event of force majeure.

10. Personal data

The Seller processes the Guest’s personal data in accordance with the privacy policy and the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan No. 94-V of 21 May 2013 “On Personal Data and Their Protection”.

11. Changes to the offer

  1. The Seller may change the offer by publishing a new version in the App and on the website https://tap.delcappuccino.kz.
  2. The new version applies to orders and subscriptions paid after its publication. An already paid subscription term continues under the previous terms until it ends.

12. Disputes

  1. The Guest sends complaints to support (contacts). The Seller responds within 10 calendar days.
  2. If a dispute is not resolved, it is settled in court under the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
