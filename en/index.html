Frequently asked questions.
When are Del Cappuccino cafés open?
4 cafés are open 24/7: Vavilon Residential Complex (247/8 Rozybakiev St), Komfort City Residential Complex (146 Serkebaev St), Promenade Business Park (44A Abay Ave), Del Cappuccino (64 Tole Bi St). Bakery to go · Rams City Residential Complex (94/12 Zhandosov St) — daily 07:00–22:00.
Where are Del Cappuccino cafés in Almaty?
5 locations in Almaty:
  • Vavilon Residential Complex (247/8 Rozybakiev St) — open 24/7
  • Komfort City Residential Complex (146 Serkebaev St) — open 24/7
  • Promenade Business Park (44A Abay Ave) — open 24/7
  • Del Cappuccino (64 Tole Bi St) — open 24/7
  • Bakery to go · Rams City Residential Complex (94/12 Zhandosov St) — daily 07:00–22:00
Is Del Cappuccino food halal?
Yes, our products are halal-certified by KMDB (halaldamu.kz).
Do you serve breakfast only in the morning?
No, breakfast is served all day — your favourite breakfasts, whatever the clock says.
How do I book a table?
Pick a café in the list of locations and message it on WhatsApp or give it a call.
Do I need to prepay a booking?
It depends on the café:
  • Vavilon Residential Complex (247/8 Rozybakiev St): VIP booths need a prepayment
  • Komfort City Residential Complex (146 Serkebaev St): a standard booking needs no prepayment
  • Del Cappuccino (64 Tole Bi St): tables need no prepayment unless you pre-order from the menu
  • Bakery to go · Rams City Residential Complex (94/12 Zhandosov St): bookings need no prepayment
Can I order food for delivery?
Yes — tap “Order in the app” to order online at delcappuccino.kz. You can also order takeaway via the café's WhatsApp.
Do you deliver?
You can order online with “Order in the app” on the website. Delivery by location:
  • Vavilon Residential Complex (247/8 Rozybakiev St): through all the delivery apps
  • Komfort City Residential Complex (146 Serkebaev St): our own delivery within Komfort City Residential Complex, plus Glovo, Yandex Eats, Choco and Wolt
  • Del Cappuccino (64 Tole Bi St): delivered by our own staff within 800 m
  • Bakery to go · Rams City Residential Complex (94/12 Zhandosov St): delivery within the residential complex
Is there parking?
  • Vavilon Residential Complex (247/8 Rozybakiev St): parking available
  • Komfort City Residential Complex (146 Serkebaev St): public parking next to the café
  • Del Cappuccino (64 Tole Bi St): city parking; VIP guests can park in the inner courtyard
  • Bakery to go · Rams City Residential Complex (94/12 Zhandosov St): parking inside the residential complex
Is there Wi-Fi?
  • Vavilon Residential Complex (247/8 Rozybakiev St): Wi-Fi available
  • Komfort City Residential Complex (146 Serkebaev St): free Wi-Fi
  • Bakery to go · Rams City Residential Complex (94/12 Zhandosov St): Wi-Fi available
Do you have a kids' area?
Yes, there's a kids' area at Vavilon Residential Complex.
What VIP rooms and banquet halls do you have?
  • Vavilon Residential Complex (247/8 Rozybakiev St): VIP rooms for 10–15 and 20–25 people, private VIP booths and a banquet hall for 30–35 people
  • Komfort City Residential Complex (146 Serkebaev St): a VIP booth
  • Promenade Business Park (44A Abay Ave): a VIP room for up to 20 people for conferences, seminars, banquets and business meetings; video on the TV screens; a main hall for up to 60; coffee breaks for 20–70 people
Can I hold a banquet at your café?
Yes, we'll host your event for a group of up to 40 people. VIP rooms work well for business breakfasts, small meetings and workshops.
Where can I work or hold a business meeting?
  • Promenade Business Park (44A Abay Ave): a space for freelancers; business dining — business lunches, working lunches and dinners; a VIP room for up to 20; video on the TV screens
Where is there a prayer room?
There's a namazkhana (prayer room) at Komfort City Residential Complex (146 Serkebaev St) and Promenade Business Park (44A Abay Ave).
Can I bring my pet?
  • Vavilon Residential Complex (247/8 Rozybakiev St): pets only on the summer terrace; large dogs must wear a muzzle
  • Bakery to go · Rams City Residential Complex (94/12 Zhandosov St): you can come with pets to pick up takeaway
Is the menu available in Kazakh and English?
Yes, you can switch the menu language on the website: ҚАЗ, РУС or ENG.
Where can I see the menu and prices?
The menu with photos and ingredients is on the website, and the full menu with prices as text is at tap.delcappuccino.kz/en/menu.
